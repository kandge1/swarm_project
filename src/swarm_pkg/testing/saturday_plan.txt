WHAT IS ALREADY BUILT AND TESTED (all offline tests green)
----------------------------------------------------------
  calibration.py --fit --channel survey
      Fits radial = c + kx*cos(b) + ky*sin(b) + kr*(r - r_bar).
      cos/sin because a world-fixed offset projects onto radial as exactly
      that; a term linear in the angle cannot represent one at all.
      Every coefficient printed against its own standard error, and anything
      under 2 se is reported "NOT measured" rather than recommended.
      Verified: recovers a planted model to 0.2 mm at sigma 0.5 mm, and
      REFUSES the real 68-row history (2 usable survey rows).

      Three filters that matter:
        - rows marked `invalid` are dropped LOUDLY (10 of them, hand-added)
        - views < 3 rejected (single-still rows get no parallax correction)
        - an EXACTLY ZERO survey error is treated as an assertion, not data.
          41 of the first 68 rows passed the same number as both
          --zone-origin and --truth-block-world, which makes survey_error
          identically zero by construction. Without this filter the fitter
          returns R^2 1.00 having measured the identity function.

  --max-clearance-mm for the nudge channel, because a nudge is only as good
      as the distance it was read from. Rows now record measure_clearance_mm
      in their provenance stanza, so a 0.5 mm caliper reading is
      distinguishable from a 5 mm illusion.

  15 SURVEY POSITIONS, all pre-flighted for reach and J1 sweep coverage
  (tightest far-corner margin is H at +13.5 mm):
      A-K  the original bearing fan, -113 to +18 deg
      L,M  (0,+7) and (3,+7) -- bearings +90 and +67, closing the documented
           +20..+90 gap. Halves the standard error on every bearing coefficient.
      N,O  (5,0) and (7,0) -- a RADIUS LADDER at bearing 0 with H, radii
           127/178/229 mm. Simulated: kr's standard error 8.2 -> 5.7 mm/m.
           Without them kr leaves 0.6 mm unresolved across the sweep, at a
           1-2 mm target.

WHY THE PLACE-ZONE BIMODALITY IS NOT WORTH FIXING
-------------------------------------------------
fit_zone computes  origin = mean(hit) - R(yaw) . mean(camera_zone),
so the origin's sensitivity to the fitted yaw is exactly |mean(camera_zone)|:

  pickup zone   mean camera offset  22.4 mm  ->  0.39 mm per degree of yaw
  place zone    mean camera offset  99.3 mm  ->  1.73 mm per degree of yaw

The pickup zone is the sweep's TARGET so its views straddle it; the place zone
is caught opportunistically from wherever the pickup sweep pointed, so its views
sit far off to one side. 4.4x the sensitivity, and a shorter yaw baseline too.

AND IT DOES NOT MATTER: the place target is a 4 in box that blocks are TOSSED
into, +-25 mm tolerance (stage 0 of the stacked-block plan). A 6 mm place survey
error is irrelevant. I nearly sent a day down this hole.
