a measurement rather than an intention (Lesson 5).

DO NOT pass --zone-origin. That would hand the survey the answer and exclude
the very term being measured -- which is how survey_error read "0.0 +- 0.0"
for the life of the project while the origin sat 28 mm out.

--position sets --truth-pickup from the table and names the row. If your tape
lands somewhere other than the nominal inches, pass the measured value instead:
  --position B --truth-pickup 0.0012 -0.1781
A taped measurement beats a nominal layout, and an explicit --truth-pickup wins.

It REFUSES to record a truth more than 30 mm from the surveyed origin, so a
mistyped position or a mat on the wrong marks stops rather than poisoning the
fit.


ORDER RATIONALE
---------------
B first (dress rehearsal). Then A..K walks the mat around the arc in small
steps, so consecutive positions are short mat moves rather than jumps across
the bench. M before L because M is on the way. N and O last: they are the
radius ladder at bearing 0 and they share H's bearing, so if you run out of
time they are the cheapest to finish tomorrow morning -- but do try, because
they are what pins the reach coefficient.

IF YOU RUN SHORT: stop wherever you are. Every position is a complete,
independent row. Drop C, E, J first if you want to prioritise. Never skip
L, M, N, O -- bearing coverage and the radius ladder are the entire point.


AFTER B, CHECK THE ROW LANDED (10 seconds)
------------------------------------------
  python3 calibration.py --fit --channel survey

Expect: "SURVEY channel: 3 usable row(s)" (A and B from this morning, plus the
new B) and "NOT ENOUGH DATA to fit -- 4 parameters need at least 6 rows."
That refusal is correct and it proves the plumbing works.

Watch for, in the run's own output:
  [survey] pickup vs taped (x, y): off by NN mm     <- the measurement
  [run] --survey-only: pickup surveyed. Nothing picked, the gripper never
        left home.                                  <- clean exit

If you instead see "Refusing to pick up a block with nowhere to put it", you
are running an older copy of the file -- that ordering bug was fixed tonight.


WHEN 6+ POSITIONS ARE IN
------------------------
  python3 calibration.py --fit --channel survey

reports radial and tangential as
    c + kx*cos(bearing) + ky*sin(bearing) + kr*(radius - r_bar)
with a standard error on every coefficient. Read the standard errors BEFORE
the coefficients: anything printed "NOT measured (< 2 se)" is a number the
data did not establish, however plausible it looks.

  all coefficients < 2 se  -> the survey bias is already inside the noise
                              across the workspace. That is a RESULT: 1.1 mm
                              generalises and tomorrow is confirmation.
  c measured, others not   -> ORIGIN_RADIAL_BIAS_M is the right SHAPE and just
                              wants its value updated. One-line change.
  kx or ky measured        -> there is a WORLD-fixed component, which
                              apply_origin_radial_bias cannot express because it
                              is radial by construction. New finding, new home.
  R^2 < 0.7                -> do not apply anything. Report the means.
